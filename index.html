Abstract

We present π², a simple, efficient, and accurate image-to-point-cloud registration paradigm. We reformulate registration as point-wise camera-centered coordinate regression: given a target image and an unaligned LiDAR point cloud, π² predicts where each sampled 3D point should lie in the target camera frame.

The predicted point set is aligned with the original sampled points through closed-form SVD-based rigid alignment, yielding the relative pose without iterative pose optimization or a learned pose-regression head. A lightweight camera-ray regularization objective provides visibility-aware supervision during training. Experiments on KITTI and nuScenes evaluate the approach under challenging outdoor conditions.
